COOKIE TRANSPARENCY

COOKIE & STORAGE POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 • Strictly Essential Cookies Only

1. DO WE USE COOKIES?

Yes, but exclusively strictly necessary, functional security cookies. FileGuard does NOT use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, social media trackers, or third-party behavioral profiling scripts.

2. COOKIES & STORAGE MECHANISMS EMPLOYED

NAME / KEY TYPE PURPOSE LIFESPAN
session HTTP Cookie Stores cryptographic CSRF session tokens to prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks. Session (browser close)
fileguard_user_uploads localStorage Maintains the client's private upload history vault and token list locally on the browser. Persistent (user clears)
fileguard_user_views localStorage Stores links received and opened from other users in your local activity vault. Persistent (user clears)
fileguard_cookie_consent localStorage Remembers your acknowledgment of this notice so the banner is not displayed again. Persistent (1 year)

3. ZERO THIRD-PARTY TRACKING

Because FileGuard does not utilize tracking, Google Analytics, Meta pixels, or advertising networks, our service operates in full compliance with the EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR standards for strictly essential functional cookies.

4. CONTROLLING YOUR STORAGE

You have complete control over local storage data. You can clear your entire transfer history at any time by navigating to My History and clicking "Clear Upload History", or via standard browser developer tools / cache settings.
