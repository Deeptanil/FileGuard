2. SERVICE LIFECYCLE & EPHEMERAL ARCHITECTURE

FileGuard is strictly an ephemeral data transit system, not a permanent backup or archiving utility:

  • Strict 12-Hour Expiration: All uploaded files and folder archives are automatically purged from cloud object storage and database catalogs after exactly 12 hours from the upload timestamp.
  • Irrevocable Purging: Once expired or manually revoked by the uploader, files cannot be recovered, restored, or reconstructed by any party, including FileGuard administrators.
  • No Account Profiling: The service does not require user accounts, passwords, or emails. File ownership is tracked solely through cryptographically random sharing tokens and client-side browser localStorage.

3. END-TO-END ENCRYPTION & DATA SECURITY

Files transmitted through FileGuard are protected via:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All HTTP data streams are secured using TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport, preventing interception or man-in-the-middle manipulation.
  • Encryption at Rest: Objects stored in Backblaze B2 cloud storage are transparently encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Cryptographic Pre-Flight Integrity: SHA-256 checksums are calculated upon upload and verified prior to download. Any tampered or corrupted block triggers an immediate HTTP 409 delivery block.
